Academy Video Editor

Academy Video Editor is a private, internal tool operated by Cam Baker (samornbaker@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

The tool signs in only to its operator's own Google account. It reads raw course videos from one Google Drive folder, edits them automatically, and saves the finished videos and descriptions back to the operator's Google Drive.

It does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and it does not access anyone else's Google account. Transcript text is sent to Google's Gemini API only to plan and generate illustrative clips and write video descriptions.
